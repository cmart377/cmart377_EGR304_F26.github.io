## Overview

This schematic is designed to support team 204's Heart Rate Monitor. Its purpose is to receive and compile information, then send it to two other PCBs. The Board shall consist of three different circuits. The first circuit is responsible for supplying power, with a blue LED indicating whether the power is on. The second circuit involves the microcontroller, and it will receive an analog signal from one board and send it to the other two boards. Finally, the third circuit is responsible for measuring the board's temperature and lighting a red LED if it gets too hot.
